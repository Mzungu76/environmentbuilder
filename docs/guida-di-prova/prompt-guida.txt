=== PROMPT (inglese — di solito funziona meglio) =============================

Character turnaround sheet, three full-body views of the SAME character in a single image:
LEFT: front view. CENTER: side view facing RIGHT. RIGHT: back view.
Follow the attached silhouette reference EXACTLY: same layout, same three equal columns,
same body proportions (about 7.5 heads tall), same neutral A-pose with arms slightly away
from the body and legs slightly apart, same feet baseline for all three figures,
each figure centered in its own third of the canvas. Do not add text, labels, props on the floor or shadows.

CHARACTER: {{PERSONAGGIO}}
OUTFIT (head to toe): {{VESTITI}}
COLOR PALETTE: {{PALETTE}}
ART STYLE: {{STILE}}

Consistency: identical face, hair, outfit, colors and proportions in all three views.
Clean, crisp outline; flat even lighting (no strong cast shadows); no motion blur.
Background: solid flat uniform {{SFONDO}} (no gradient, no floor, no scenery).
Canvas aspect ratio 2:1 (3072x1536).

NEGATIVE: extra limbs, cropped feet, different outfit between views, text, watermark,
perspective distortion, dynamic pose, crossed arms, background scenery, cast shadow.

{{PERSONAGGIO}}  es.: "male cave explorer, mid 30s, short curly brown hair, light stubble, friendly smile"
{{VESTITI}}      es.: "yellow hard hat with headlamp, beige rolled-sleeve shirt, red neckerchief, olive cargo pants with knee pads,
                  brown hiking boots, fingerless black gloves, leather belt with carabiner, canvas backpack with pickaxe"
{{PALETTE}}      es.: "warm earthy tones: beige, olive green, brown, accents of yellow and red"
{{STILE}}        es.: "hand-painted cartoon illustration, thick clean outline, soft cel shading, game character art"
{{SFONDO}}       es.: "magenta (#FF00FF)"   oppure   "white"

Foglio di rotazione del personaggio: tre viste a figura intera dello STESSO personaggio in un'unica immagine:
SINISTRA fronte, CENTRO profilo verso DESTRA, DESTRA retro. Segui ESATTAMENTE la sagoma di riferimento allegata:
stesso layout a tre colonne uguali, stesse proporzioni (circa 7,5 teste), stessa posa neutra "A" con braccia leggermente

Niente testi, etichette o ombre. Stesso volto, capelli, abiti e colori nelle tre viste. Contorno netto, luce uniforme,
sfondo piatto di un solo colore. Formato 2:1.

=== SE IL TUO STRUMENTO NON REGGE IL FOGLIO UNICO (modalità B) ==============
1) Genera solo il FRONTE: usa solo la cella sinistra della guida (ritaglia 1024x1536) e lo stesso prompt con "single front view".
2) Poi genera il PROFILO destro e il RETRO passando come riferimenti: la cella corrispondente della guida + il fronte già
   generato, con la frase: "same character as the reference image, same outfit and colors, now in side view facing right".
