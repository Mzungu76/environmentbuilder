=== PROMPT (inglese) ==========================================================

Two full-body poses of the SAME character as the reference character image, side by side in one image:
LEFT: climbing pose. RIGHT: jumping pose. Both seen from the SIDE, facing RIGHT.
Follow the attached silhouette reference closely: same two equal columns, each figure centered in its half of the canvas.
LEFT (climbing): body upright, both hands reaching up and forward as if gripping a rope or ladder above the head,
near knee raised forward, far leg extended below.
RIGHT (jumping): mid-air, torso slightly leaning forward, both arms stretched up and forward, both knees tucked up.

Keep the exact same face, hair, outfit, accessories, colors, proportions and art style as the reference character:
{{STILE}}.
Clean, crisp outline, flat even lighting, no motion blur, no cast shadows, no scenery, no ground, no rope/ladder/wall drawn.
Background: solid flat uniform {{SFONDO}}. Canvas aspect ratio 4:3 (2048x1536).

NEGATIVE: different outfit, different face, extra limbs, cropped hands or feet, text, watermark, scenery, ground, ladder,
rope, perspective distortion, background gradient.
